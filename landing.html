How It Works

Simple. Fast. Secure.

Three steps is all it takes to report or recover a lost item on campus.

1
Report It

Lost or found something? Create a post with a photo, description, and location in under a minute. Your report is visible to the entire campus community instantly.

2
Browse & Match

Search by category, keyword, or location. If you recognise a found item as yours, submit a claim with a description of why it belongs to you.

3
Reunite

Campus admins review all claims and verify the rightful owner. Once confirmed, you're notified and can arrange collection. Simple, fair, and secure.

Why It Works

Everything you need to recover what's yours

Designed from the ground up for the campus environment.

Account-Protected Access

Only registered campus users can view reports and submit claims, keeping the system trusted and abuse-free.

Real-Time Listings

Posts go live instantly. Search and filter the entire campus report board by item type, location, or keyword.

Location Tagging

Tag the exact building, hall, or area where an item was lost or found to help narrow down the search.

Admin Oversight

Every claim goes through a human review. Admins verify ownership descriptions before confirming, preventing false claims.

Photo Evidence

Upload photos when reporting an item. Visual proof helps others recognise their belongings and speeds up the matching process.

Claim Tracking

Track all your submitted claims in one place. See whether your claim is pending review, confirmed, or if the item was awarded to another claimant.
